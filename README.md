# YouTube Music - Hide Video & Custom Themes

![Less video. More music. — YouTube Music Hide Video & Custom Themes by Ameer Jamal](docs/promotional/youtube-music-extension-hero.png)

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/Ameer-Jamal/youtube-music-hide-video-custom-themes/releases/latest) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/Ameer-Jamal/youtube-music-hide-video-custom-themes/issues)

A focused YouTube Music experience by Ameer Jamal. Hide the video, make room for your queue, and choose colors that fit your mood.

26 light and dark themes, original and custom palettes, optional wide home layout, custom canvas/player/accent colors, live updates across Music tabs, a full-width queue when video is hidden, always-visible volume, a live, mute-aware volume percentage, adjustable spacing, optional promo hiding, and validated JSON backups.

**Hide video is visual only:** playback continues and streaming data use is unchanged. It does not bypass YouTube Premium or force Song mode.

## Install

1. [Download the latest release](https://github.com/Ameer-Jamal/youtube-music-hide-video-custom-themes/releases/latest) and extract the ZIP into a folder you will keep.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` or `edge://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
3. Choose **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder containing `manifest.json`.
4. Refresh YouTube Music, then open the extension to choose a theme and adjust your listening space.

Chrome Web Store installation is coming soon. Chrome, Edge, and other Chromium browsers can use the downloadable package now.

## Make it yours

Choose a light or dark theme, or create your own with Canvas, Player, and Accent colors. Changes apply to your open YouTube Music tabs.

Hide the video to give your queue more room. Switch on **Wide home layout** if you prefer shelves to fill more of the screen; it is off by default. Adjust volume controls, queue width, and spacing to suit you.

Use **Export settings** to save a backup and **Import settings** to restore it. The power switch turns customizations off, and **Reset** restores the defaults.

## Updating

Download and extract the latest ZIP into your existing extension folder, then click **Reload** on the extension’s card in your browser and refresh YouTube Music. Check the version at the bottom of the popup to confirm the update.

## Help and feedback

Use **Report a bug** or **Request a feature** in the extension, or [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/Ameer-Jamal/youtube-music-hide-video-custom-themes/issues).

Preferences sync through your browser. The extension does not collect analytics. Read the [privacy policy](docs/PRIVACY.md) for details.
