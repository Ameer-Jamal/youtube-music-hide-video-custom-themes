# Chrome Web Store listing

Name: YouTube Music - Hide Video & Custom Themes

Summary: Hide the video, give your queue room to breathe, and make YouTube Music yours with custom themes and player controls.

## Description

Your music deserves its own space. Turn YouTube Music into a focused listening experience with a full-width queue and a palette that fits your mood.

- Hide the video panel while music keeps playing.
- Choose eight palettes: YouTube original, Material dark, Galaxy purple, Midnight blue, Crimson night, Emerald shadow, Pure black, or your own colors.
- Customize canvas, player, and accent colors with a live preview.
- Keep volume visible and show its percentage.
- Adjust queue width, spacing, and volume slider width.
- Optionally hide upgrade banners.
- Back up settings with JSON export and import.
- Disable customizations instantly with the power switch.

No analytics. No remote code. Runs only on YouTube Music. Preferences sync through your browser.

Hiding video changes the interface only. It does not force an audio-only stream, reduce bandwidth, or bypass Premium requirements. This independent extension is not affiliated with Google or YouTube.
