# Privacy policy

YouTube Music - Hide Video & Custom Themes does not collect, transmit, sell, or analyze personal data. It has no analytics, advertising SDKs, remote code, or external server connections.

Theme and layout preferences are saved using Chrome storage sync. If browser sync is enabled, Google may synchronize these settings across your signed-in browsers under its own privacy policy. Settings contain colors and preference switches, not listening history or account information.

The content script runs only on https://music.youtube.com/. It adds presentation styles and does not read your playlists, messages, credentials, or browsing history. Imported settings are processed locally. Exported settings are downloaded only when requested.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/Ameer-Jamal/youtube-music-hide-video-custom-themes/issues.
